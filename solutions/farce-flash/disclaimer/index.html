Full disclaimer

F.A.R.C.E. Flash is a joke. Farce Systems is a satirical project by Joshua Hickson. Everything on the campaign page is written in the voice of a hardware startup that sincerely believes it can replace 128 GB of unified memory with forty consumer MicroSD cards. It cannot. That is the point.

The Feasibility Report is real, and it says so. It was generated with AI research tooling and published without edits to its content, tone, or conclusion. It concludes that the architecture achieves "less than 5% of the performance of the hardware it sought to disrupt" and that the array destroys its own storage media in roughly 64 days. We fixed typographic artifacts from the source export and nothing else. It is not a peer-reviewed document and its citations have not been independently verified. Read it as what it is: an argument against the product it analyzes.

Farce Systems is not a company. It is not registered, incorporated, or capitalized. It has no employees. The "two engineers" on the campaign page are fictional. One real person sources parts, packs boxes, and answers email.

What you would actually receive. A box of off-the-shelf electronic components, exactly as itemized on the kit page, and a single-sheet bill of materials. Every part is a commodity item with a public datasheet. There is no printed circuit board, no enclosure, no assembled hardware, and no software in any kit. Nothing you receive will run a language model. Nothing you receive is a computer. Assembly, if you attempt it, is entirely your own project.

No performance is promised. Any speed, capacity, or capability figure anywhere on this site describes what the architecture targets, not what any kit does. Every one of those figures is published alongside the analysis that contradicts it.

OpenFARCE does not exist yet. It is described as pre-alpha because it is unwritten. It is not included in, or required by, anything sold here.

Tiers are capped, and the caps are real. Each physical tier closes at a fixed number of units — 100, 40, 20, 10, 25, and 50 — because one person fronts the cost of every box from a fixed parts budget and can only afford to be wrong about so many of them.

Refunds. Full refund on request at any time before your kit ships. Parts are ordered only after payment clears, so there is always a window. After shipping, anything missing, incorrect, or dead on arrival is replaced or refunded. If parts pricing moves enough to make a tier unfulfillable at its listed contents, everyone affected is offered a refund before any parts are ordered.

NVIDIA and the DGX Spark. The NVIDIA DGX Spark is a real product made by NVIDIA Corporation. It is a good one. Farce Systems has no affiliation with, endorsement from, or relationship to NVIDIA. It is referenced here for comparison and criticized nowhere.
